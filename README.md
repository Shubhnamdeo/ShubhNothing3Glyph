# ✨ Glyph Matrix Suite – Nothing Phone (3)

A versatile utility and review suite built for the **Nothing Phone (3) 25×25 Glyph Matrix**, designed to deliver interactive information, flash reviews, and custom displays directly to the rear LED matrix without needing to wake your screen.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 💡 **Interactive Glyph Matrix (25×25)**: Direct rendering on the Nothing Phone (3) dot matrix LED display.
- 🔼 **Hardware Button Controls**: Navigate and interact using physical volume buttons without turning on your screen.
- 💬 **Custom Text & Scrolling Display**: Smooth text tickers with auto-scrolling for long messages and multi-language characters.
- 🎧 **Media & Audio Integration**: Optional synced audio playback.
- ⚡ **Roadmap & Expansion**: Designed to expand with more Glyph widgets, tools, tickers, animations, and custom interaction modes.

---

## 🎮 How It Works

1. **Launch the App** and choose your review deck or custom display mode.
2. **Navigate with Volume Buttons**:
   - ▶️ **Short press Volume**: Toggle / Flip display
   - 🔼 **Volume Up**: Affirmative action / Mark as correct ✅
   - 🔽 **Volume Down**: Mark as review again ❌
3. **Glyph Matrix Display**: Dynamic rendering with custom font scaling and automatic marquee scrolling.

## ⚙️ Initial Setup & Tips

- 💤 **First-time Display**: If the Glyph matrix doesn't wake up immediately on first start, lock and unlock your screen once or cycle your Glyph Toys to initialize the hardware matrix driver.
- 🔠 **Font Sizing**: Customize font sizing and scroll speeds directly in the Settings menu.
- 📱 Designed specifically for the **Nothing Phone (3)** Glyph Matrix.
